Hadibur

As part of my work at BenZakai studio, I created social media videos for various organizations, including the "HaDibur" ("The Speech") initiative. The "HaDibur" initiative was founded by bereaved families after October 7th, with the goal of cleaning up toxic public discourse in Israel, with an emphasis on members of Knesset and journalists. Sarit Zussman, mother of reservist Master Sergeant Ben Zussman, is one of the initiative's prominent figures. Sarit is frequently interviewed by the media, and my role was to edit the audio of the interviews, plan a storyboard for the visuals that would accompany Sarit's words, and produce videos to promote the HaDibur initiative on Instagram and TikTok.
