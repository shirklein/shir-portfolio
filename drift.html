Drift

Digital Nomad App (Bezalel course)

The Task:
As part of the "Motion for Digital Product" course in my fourth year at Bezalel, I was tasked with creating a moving graphic package for an app aimed at digital nomads - people who move from place to place while working remotely. The brief required a loading screen, 3 onboarding questions, and a pop-up screen, with the onboarding questions designed to help the app understand user preferences and suggest an ideal destination.

My Solution:
I built the motion language around the recurring rhythm of the digital nomad's life - unpacking and settling in upon arrival, then packing up again before moving to the next destination. This principle of repeated packing/unpacking motion guided the animation across the loading screen, onboarding flow, and pop-up.
