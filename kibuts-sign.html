Kibbutz Signage

Stop Motion Paper Cut Animation Visual Storytelling

Created as a stop-motion signage system for three businesses in a kibbutz — a cowshed, fishery and chicken coop.

Each sign reveals its name through an action drawn from the place itself, using hand-cut paper and frame-by-frame animation.

The Kibbutz

The three animated signs were designed as part of a larger illustrated map of the kibbutz.

Built frame by frame

Each sign was built from hand-cut paper and animated frame by frame, using physical movement to turn the typography itself into part of the story.
