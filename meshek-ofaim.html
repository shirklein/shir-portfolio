Ofaim Farm "Ident" Video (Bezalel course)

The Task:
As part of the third-year "Composition in Motion" course in the Visual Communication department at Bezalel Academy of Art and Design, I was asked to choose a beloved brand and create a short "Ident" video expressing its atmosphere and values. I chose Ofaim Farm for Sustainable Agriculture, which grows and prepares all the goodness of the land - fruits, vegetables, eggs, jams, cheeses, breads, oil, and more - through a slow, patient, handcrafted process that honors the animals, the earth, and the people who work it.

My Solution:
In the spirit of the farm's values, I chose to work with hand-painted markers, frame by frame. The process included: planning the video's flow through a storyboard; building a three-dimensional environment in Spline; recording a "skeleton" video within that 3D environment; breaking the skeleton video down into individual frames and printing them; manually coloring all 300 frames using markers and a light table; scanning the colored frames; and finally editing everything together in Adobe After Effects.
