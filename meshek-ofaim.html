OFaim Farm

Concept Branding Art Direction 3D Design & Animation Hand-Drawn Animation Compositing

An ident film created for Ofaim Farm, inspired by the farm's slow-food philosophy, local production and hands-on approach to making.

I developed a fictional marker brand as the visual concept for the ident, using it to translate the farm's values into a playful visual language.

Slow food called for slow creation

Building the Foundation

I designed, built and animated the entire scene in 3D using Spline, creating the structural foundation for the final film.

From Digital to Handmade

I printed 300 frames from the 3D animation and drew over each one by hand with markers. The frames were then scanned and brought back into After Effects, transforming the clean digital foundation into something tactile, imperfect and handmade.
