Three Genres in Motion

Frame-by-Frame Animation Illustration Mixed Media

For this hand-drawn animation series, I used footwear as a visual shorthand for three musical genres: sneakers for hip-hop, boots for country and heels for tango. Each pair was animated to reflect the movement of its genre, using a different drawing technique to match its energy — markers, ink and brush, and charcoal.
