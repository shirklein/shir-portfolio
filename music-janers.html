Spotify Cover Animations - Sneakers, Boots, and Heels (Pastiche Course)

The Task:
As part of a pastiche course at Bezalel, I was tasked with creating 3 Spotify covers for three different musical genres.

My Solution:
I chose to express each genre through the pair of shoes that characterizes it - sneakers for hip-hop, boots for country, and heels for tango. I animated each pair of shoes according to the dance style matching its genre, using a drawing technique suited to the energy of that style: the hip-hop cover was drawn with markers, the country cover with ink and brush, and the tango cover with charcoal. All the videos were hand-drawn, frame by frame.
