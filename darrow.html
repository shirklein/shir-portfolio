Darrow "Bring Your Kids to Work Day" (BenZakai Studio)

The Task:
Following the videos I created for the HaDibur initiative, the Israeli company Darrow reached out to the studio and requested videos in a similar style. They were planning a "Bring Your Kids to Work Day" and wanted to create content presenting the day on LinkedIn and similar platforms. During the day, they interviewed the children and asked them questions about their parents' jobs - but wanted the content produced without showing the children's faces.

My Solution:
I took all the raw interview footage and edited it into coherent content. To avoid showing video of the children being interviewed, they had the kids draw pictures related to their interview answers - I took those drawings and animated them into visuals that accompanied the audio. The result was two videos.
