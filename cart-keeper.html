Cart Keeper

"Cart Keeper" App Marketing Video (BenZakai Studio)

The Task:
"Cart Keeper" is an app that allows online store owners to send WhatsApp reminders to customers who started filling a cart but abandoned it before completing their purchase. As part of my work at BenZakai studio, I needed to create a marketing video that would quickly and clearly explain why store owners should download the app.

My Solution:
I developed the storyboard concept myself, together with AI, building the video around the app's mascot character - shown reminding shoppers to complete their purchase, and as the figure driving store owners' profit graphs upward with percentage gains. I then animated the character using Gemini and embedded it into After Effects compositions.
