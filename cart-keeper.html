Cart Keeper — Product Film

Concept Storyboarding AI Production Campaign Assets

CartKeeper was already a live product with an established brand character, helping e-commerce stores recover abandoned carts.

My role was to develop a new promotional film around that existing character — translating the product's functionality into a simple visual story.

What if the Keeper could physically chase down an abandoned cart?

Cart recovery is mostly invisible. I wanted to turn that automated process into a physical, character-driven story.

Abandon → Pursue → Recover

I turned cart recovery into a physical chase: the Keeper catches abandoned carts and pushes them back up the customer’s revenue graph.

From Storyboard to Film

The storyboard established the key actions and compositions, which I then developed into the final animated scenes.
