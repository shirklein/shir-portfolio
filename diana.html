Diana

Editorial Design Art Direction Typography Print Design

A fashion magazine spread built as a retrospective on Princess Diana as an enduring style icon, designed for a second-year editorial course in the Visual Communication department at Bezalel Academy.

Each spread looks at a different piece of her image — dresses, jewelry, hats, swimwear, bags and shoes — pairing archival photography with a custom display typeface and a restrained, catalog-like layout system.

Dressed in White

The opening spreads trace her wardrobe from the wedding gown onward, using a simple two-column grid so the archival photography carries the page.

Fit for a Queen

Jewelry, hair and the crown itself get their own catalog-style treatment, close-up product shots set against portrait photography.

Why? A Hat!

A dedicated index page catalogs her hats by year and auction estimate, styled after a fashion-house lookbook.

Cinderella

Bags and shoes close out the piece the same way they opened it — as collectible objects, each logged with its year, condition and price.
