Diana

Editorial Design Typography Print Design

An editorial publication exploring Princess Diana's iconic fashion and personal style, created as part of a second-year editorial design course at Bezalel Academy.

Each section focuses on a different element of Diana's wardrobe — from dresses and jewelry to hats, bags and shoes — using archival photography, typography and layout to create a cohesive editorial system.
